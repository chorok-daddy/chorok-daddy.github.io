Human-Following Trailer

Force sensing and model-based control for assisted trailer motion.

← Research

Robotics & Autonomy

2021-08-05 – 2021-12-20 · Completed

Korea Railroad Research Institute (KRRI)

Overview

The study combines sensing and control to assist a trailer pulled by a person or bicycle under changing load and slope.

Approach

  • Estimate coupling force through spring displacement and position sensing.
  • Use estimated state, load and slope to calculate motor commands.
  • Connect actuator analysis, mechanical design and embedded control with planned tests under varied conditions.

Intended contribution

Reduce perceived inertia and resistance through joint sensor-mechanism and control design, including modular construction and adjustable mounting height.
