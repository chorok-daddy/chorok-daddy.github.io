## Undergraduate internship eligibility

Undergraduates with at least two semesters remaining before graduation may apply year-round.

## Periods and participation

- Summer intake: July–December
- Winter intake: January–June
- Research facilities and lab academic activities
- Compensation based on role and responsibilities

## How to apply

Select **Undergraduate internship** in the form and include your university, major, year, expected graduation date, preferred participation period and research interests.

For graduate inquiries, select **Graduate research** and include your research interests and intended entry term.
