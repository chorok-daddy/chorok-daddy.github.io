## Undergraduate internship eligibility

Undergraduates with at least two semesters remaining before graduation may apply at any time.

## Periods and participation

- Summer intake: July–December
- Winter intake: January–June
- Access to research infrastructure and lab academic activities.
- Compensation depends on the role and responsibilities.

## How to apply

1. Open the application and inquiry form below.
2. Select **Undergraduate internship** as the inquiry type.
3. Enter your name and reply email.
4. In your message, include your university and major, current year and expected graduation date, preferred participation period, and research interests.

For graduate research inquiries, select **Graduate research** and include your research interests and intended entry term.

[Inquiry & application form]({{CONTACT_FORM}})

The form supports English and Korean and does not require a Google account. Submissions are not public. Provide only information needed for your inquiry; do not include sensitive personal information or confidential materials.
