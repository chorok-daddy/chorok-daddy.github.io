## Overview

The 2016–2018 project connects automated responses, consultation assistance and promotion support to the Qsales online sales platform.

## Approach

- Collect and prepare customer-contact data for sales tasks.
- Integrate assistance into chat, product explanation, payment, appointment and form workflows.
- Train on the data and tasks of sales consultations.

## Intended contribution

Use operational consultation data as a basis for subsequent learning and assistance within customer–agent interactions.
