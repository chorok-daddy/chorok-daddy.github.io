## Overview

RGB-D perception estimates pallet pose and guides movement, rotation and fork insertion for vehicle-mounted loading equipment. The project family spans infrastructure design, prototypes, embedded automation and later robustness work in 2022–2025.

## Results

- The 2024 report records successful control through fork insertion for ten pallet types across fourteen test scenarios.
- Converted object detections and depth into relative position, angle and distance for motion commands.
- Integrated remote operation, task assistance, image acquisition and communication interfaces.

## Contributions

Connected visual perception to physical task execution on compact logistics equipment, with both operator control and automatic guidance.

## Evaluation scope

The results apply to the 2024 test conditions. The 2025 task covers further validation under changing lighting, ground and vehicle-mounted conditions.

*Sources: 2024 embedded automation module report; 2025 advanced validation task specification.*
