## Research

Integrated RGB-camera and LiDAR perception with loading-decision interfaces and built point-cloud data for cargo and container space.

## Results

Certified test results reported in 2022.

| Task | Result | Test conditions |
|---|---|---|
| Position and volume perception | mIoU 0.867 | Ten unseen normal cargo types · 100 trials |
| Cargo classification and barcode | 100% joint success | Both functions had to succeed |
| Damage recognition | 94% success | Normal and damaged cargo groups · 100 trials |
