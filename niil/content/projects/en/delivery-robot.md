## Overview

An edge module reads carrier information, barcodes and cargo damage and communicates the results to a delivery robot and its management system. Related contracts span 2022–2024.

## Results

- Integrated cameras, lighting, GPU processing and ROS communication inside the cargo compartment.
- The 2024 report records 100% recognition in an internal repeated test: six shipping-label types, each inserted ten times.
- Built data in a simulated compartment reflecting the camera placement and lighting of the target system.

## Contributions

Connected cargo perception to task instructions and delivery confirmation, integrating carrier identification, damage recognition and barcode decoding with robot operations.

## Evaluation scope

The 100% result is limited to the internal test conditions. Formal testing was listed as planned in the report.

*Source: 2024 delivery-robot task instruction and delivery confirmation module report, chapter 3.*
