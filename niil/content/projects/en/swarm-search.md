## Overview

The study adapts particle swarm optimization to vehicle dynamics and spatial constraints, allowing a group of unmanned vehicles to share information while searching for a target.

## Simulation results

| Measure | Final report result |
|---|---|
| Search space | 1,000,000 m³ virtual environment |
| Swarm size | Fifteen simulated vehicles |
| Problems | Nine search problems |
| Search duration | Within fifty updates |
| Failure rate | 0.55% |
| Mean position error | 11.4 cm |

These are simulation results, not field-flight results for fifteen drones.

## Contributions

- Coupled optimization updates with vehicle motion and spatial constraints.
- Used individual observations to guide collective search.
- Combined a virtual environment, swarm model, test problems and repeated evaluation.
