## Overview

The project represents temporal gestures as composite images sampled from ordinary camera video and classifies them with deep neural networks.

## Results

- Built training and test data for twelve gestures through regular frame sampling and composition.
- Adapted a VGG16 network using pretrained weights.
- The 2018 annual report describes near-100% accuracy for eight-, ten- and twelve-gesture classification with different people, clothing and backgrounds in the test data.

## Contributions

Combined compact representations of temporal movement with evaluation outside the training capture conditions. Further work targeted gesture-region extraction, sequential commands, automated data generation and IoT responses.

## Evaluation scope

These are limited first-year classification experiments reported in 2018, not full sign-language sentence translation or a guarantee of final IoT service performance.

*Source: 2018 annual progress and plan report, research results and evaluation sections.*
