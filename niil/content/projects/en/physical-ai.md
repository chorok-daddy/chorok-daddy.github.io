## Research

- Design multilevel workspaces around robot joint freedom and vertical reach.
- Learn an initial action space from demonstrations, then optimize task order and paths with reinforcement learning.
- Validate Sim-to-Real transfer through simulation, miniature systems and full-scale equipment.

## Research targets

| Metric | Target |
|---|---|
| Demonstration space | Robot café within approximately 3.3 m² |
| Footprint | At least 50% smaller than a conventional commercial robot café |
| Throughput per unit area | 30% improvement |
| Technology readiness | TRL 2 → TRL 5 |
