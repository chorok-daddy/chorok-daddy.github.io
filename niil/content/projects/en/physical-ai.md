## Overview

This five-year study co-designs compact workspaces and robot learning, using vertical work surfaces and redundant joints to improve productivity in tight spaces.

## Research directions

- Co-design workspace geometry and motion to reduce unused space and avoid contact.
- Learn a safe action region from demonstrations, then improve task sequences and paths through reinforcement learning.
- Progress from simulation to miniature systems and full-scale hardware to separate kinematic and dynamic challenges.

## Targets

| Measure | Planned target |
|---|---|
| Demonstration | Robot café within approximately 3.3 m² |
| Footprint | At least 50% less area than a conventional commercial robot café |
| Throughput density | 30% improvement in operations per minute per unit area |
| Readiness | TRL 2 to TRL 5 |

These are research targets, not achieved results. The work progresses from spatial and kinematic modeling to learning, miniature validation, real-world adaptation and integrated demonstration.

*Source: 2026 agreement research plan. Overall period: 2026–2031; the administrative record covers the first year.*
