Visual Intelligence & Logistics

2026-10-01 – 2026-12-15 · Ongoing

Korea Railroad Research Institute (KRRI)

Overview

This KRRI contract has been in progress since October 2026. It develops a vision module providing object geometry and tray occupancy for robotic cargo handling.

Planned contributions

  • Use RGB-D and RGB images to estimate cargo position, orientation and picking information.
  • Identify occupancy of moving trays for downstream operations.
  • Record sensor inputs and decisions to support replay-based verification.

Current stage

The description follows the technical proposal. Performance targets are not presented as completed results; evaluation results will be added when available.
