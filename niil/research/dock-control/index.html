Embedded Dock Control

Sensor-driven embedded controllers for dock automation.

← Research

Robotics & Autonomy

2015-07-20 – 2016-12-26 · Completed

Daehan Spray System · Korea Railroad Research Institute (KRRI)

Overview

Related contracts in 2015 and 2016 cover a microprocessor board and an MCU-based dock controller. They have different sponsors and are not represented as one continuous grant.

Contributions

  • The 2015 material records circuit and board design alongside firmware for sensor inputs.
  • State-based operation covers vehicle entry, stopping, exit and manual control.
  • The 2016 scope implements existing dock functions in a compact MCU-based system.

Application

Connect sensors, a controller and actuators in a compact structure suitable for dock installation.

Project record and sources

Principal investigator: Ki-Baek Lee
