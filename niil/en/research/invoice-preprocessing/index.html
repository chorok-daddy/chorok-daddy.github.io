Design of an Edge Device-Based Shipping Label Image Preprocessing Module (Translation)

Perspective and orientation correction for shipping-label images.

← Research

Visual Intelligence & Logistics

2024-07-22 – 2024-12-02 · Completed

Korea Railroad Research Institute (KRRI)

English title is a translation; no official English title was verified.

Overview

The work prepares oblique and rotated shipping-label images for OCR and document-understanding models.

Results

  • Detected label regions and applied inverse perspective transformation.
  • Compared OCR outputs across rotation candidates to select a reading orientation.
  • Selected useful label images using criteria including the presence of Korean text.

Contributions

Addressed capture-quality issues before recognition, linking geometric correction and orientation alignment with dataset preparation.

Evaluation scope

The report includes perspective correction and 90°, 180° and 270° rotation examples. It does not establish a quantified improvement in OCR accuracy.

Project record and sources

Kwangwoon University principal investigator: Ki-Baek Lee
