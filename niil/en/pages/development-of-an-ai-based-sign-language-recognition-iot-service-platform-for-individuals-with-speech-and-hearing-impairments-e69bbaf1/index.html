IoT service platform for people with language disorder based on AI sign language recognition

CNN sign-gesture recognition using composite images of nine frames.

← Research

Human-Centered AI

2017-03-01 – 2020-02-29 · Completed

National Research Foundation of Korea (NRF)

Results

  • Classified sign gestures with CNNs using composite images of nine video frames.
  • Designed gesture-region extraction and similarity networks for distance changes and consecutive gestures.
  • Developed semi-automatic training-data generation across people, clothing and backgrounds.

Criteria reported as met in the 2020 completion report.
