Development of quantitative emotion-sensibility evaluation model of non-face-to-face environment users and commercialization of interactive digital content application

Video, speech and text models for emotion and engagement analysis.

← Research

Human-Centered AI

2023-04-01 – 2025-12-31 · Completed

Institute of Information & Communications Technology Planning & Evaluation (IITP)

NTIS link

Overview

The project combines video, speech and text to estimate physiological signals, emotion, engagement and anomalous behavior. NiiL contributed rPPG signal analysis, speech/text emotion models, gaze-based engagement, full-body anomaly detection and shared evaluation environments.

Results

Measure Reported result Scope
Video-based heart rate MAE 2.53 bpm Joint consortium result
Video-based HRV MAE 24.58 ms Joint consortium result
Speech/text emotion 85.9% accuracy Internal model evaluation using IEMOCAP and MELD
Engagement prediction 70.4% Project evaluation metric
Full-body anomaly detection 76.0% accuracy Project evaluation metric

Speech/text accuracy increased from 83.8% in year two to 85.9% in year three, a gain of 2.1 percentage points. Metrics and datasets differ across tasks.

Contributions

  • Combined temporal, frequency and spatial rPPG representations, including CSP-based spatial features.
  • Used contrastive speech/text learning to model emotional relationships in conversations.
  • Contributed to shared demonstration environments including the LucyCare kiosk; product integration, commercialization and living-lab evaluation were joint work.

Evaluation scope

These are research results reported in the final evaluation, not clinical diagnostic accuracy or guaranteed service performance.
