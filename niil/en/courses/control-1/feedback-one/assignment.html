Week 9 · Closed-loop Gain and Sensitivity

English translation

Before practice

01 Why measure results?

The same action can produce different results when surroundings or the device change. How could measuring actual results help compensate for this?

Description
  • Measure the output and adjust the action according to its difference from the target
  • Reduce the effect of model gain changes on the overall response through appropriate design

02 How much to adjust the control action

Would a large control action for even a small error give more accurate control? What problems might arise when increasing the response?

Description
  • Stability, measurement noise, and actuation limits
  • Improved sensitivity alone does not establish the suitability of the full design
