2. Baseline gain and sensitivity

Sensitivity is the ratio of relative changes for a very small gain change. For a finite change such as +10%, the predicted change may differ slightly from recalculating the original function. Differentiating the overall gain T=Ka/(1+beta*Ka) gives dT/dKa=1/(1+beta*Ka)^2. Substituting into S=(Ka/T)*(dT/dKa) gives S=1/(1+beta*Ka). For a small change, ΔT≈(dT/dKa)ΔKa, hence ΔT/T≈S*(ΔKa/Ka). Below, approx uses this expression, while exact directly compares T before and after the change.
Prediction before running: ⟦enter here⟧
Ka=5000;beta=.05;
Topen=Ka;Tclosed=Ka/(1+Ka*beta);
Sopen=1;Sclosed=1/(1+Ka*beta);
disp(table(Topen,Tclosed,Sopen,Sclosed));

3. Verify relative changes

The relative change is (gain after change−gain before change)/gain before change. Check that you are not dividing by the changed gain.
Prediction before running: ⟦enter here⟧

T0=Ka/(1+Ka*beta);T1=(Ka*(1+delta))/(1+Ka*(1+delta)*beta);
exact=(T1-T0)/T0;approx=delta/(1+Ka*beta);
disp(table(exact,approx));
