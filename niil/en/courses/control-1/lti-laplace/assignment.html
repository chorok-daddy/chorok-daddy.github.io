Week 3 · From Linearity to the Laplace Transform

English translation

Before practice

01 Use known responses

If you know the motion caused by an applied force, can you predict the motion when its magnitude changes or several forces act together?

Description
  • Superposition applies to linear models
  • Match conditions when comparing input responses, for example by setting initial conditions to 0

02 Why express an equation in another form?

A differential equation describes changes over time. Could expressing the same relation in another form make calculations or system connections easier?

Description
  • Convert time relations into algebraic relations in the s-domain
  • Use for model connections and response calculations. Account for initial conditions separately.
