Week 10 · DC Motor Disturbance Rejection

English translation

Before practice

01 A system subject to external effects

An external force acts on a DC motor whose angular velocity should remain constant. Why is control needed even when the target does not change?

Description
  • Effect of disturbance torque on output ω
  • Distinguish target-tracking performance from disturbance rejection

02 Responding to external effects

If the external force itself cannot be removed, what can reduce the angular velocity deviation?

Description
  • Adjust the control action through feedback from angular velocity measurements
  • Distinguish removing the disturbance itself from reducing its effect on the output
