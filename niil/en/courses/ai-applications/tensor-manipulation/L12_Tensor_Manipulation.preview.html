1 Tensor in pytorch

  • Tensor?
    • Vector (Rank = 1)
      [123]\begin{bmatrix}1&2&3\end{bmatrix}
    • Matrix (Rank = 2) [123456]\begin{bmatrix}1&2&3\\4&5&6\end{bmatrix}
    • Tensor (Rank > 2)
      • Difficult to display on a 2D screen
      • Possible to visualize mentally, though ability varies
    • We recommend reviewing the mathematical definitions of rank, shape, and axis whenever you have time
    • Many related YouTube videos are available

2 Tensor Combinations

  • Concatenation
    • Join arrays by removing their boundaries. [1234](⋅)[5678]→[12563478]\begin{bmatrix} 1 & 2 \\ 3 & 4 \end{bmatrix} (\cdot) \begin{bmatrix} 5 & 6 \\ 7 & 8 \end{bmatrix} \rightarrow \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 2 & 5 & 6 \\ 3 & 4 & 7 & 8 \end{bmatrix}
x = torch.FloatTensor([[1, 2], [3, 4]])
y = torch.FloatTensor([[5, 6], [7, 8]])
print(x)
print(y)
print(torch.cat([x,y], dim = 0))
print(torch.cat([x,y], dim = 1))
tensor([[1., 2.],
        [3., 4.]])
tensor([[5., 6.],
        [7., 8.]])
tensor([[1., 2.],
        [3., 4.],
        [5., 6.],
        [7., 8.]])
tensor([[1., 2., 5., 6.],
        [3., 4., 7., 8.]])
  • Stack
    • Join arrays while keeping their boundaries distinct. [1234](*)[5678]→[[1234][5678]]\begin{bmatrix} 1 & 2 \\ 3 & 4 \end{bmatrix} (*) \begin{bmatrix} 5 & 6 \\ 7 & 8 \end{bmatrix} \rightarrow \begin{bmatrix} \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 2 \\ 3 & 4 \end{bmatrix} & \begin{bmatrix} 5 & 6 \\ 7 & 8 \end{bmatrix} \end{bmatrix}
print(torch.stack([x,y], dim = 0))
print(torch.stack([x,y], dim = 1))
tensor([[[1., 2.],
         [3., 4.]],

        [[5., 6.],
         [7., 8.]]])
tensor([[[1., 2.],
         [5., 6.]],

        [[3., 4.],
         [7., 8.]]])

z = x.reshape(2, -1) # [2, 12]
